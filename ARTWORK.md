# Background artwork

Path: `assets/images/manga_background.png`

Original manga/manhwa inspired illustration made for this project with the
built-in image generation tool. It depicts original fictional characters, not
screenshots from a published manga or manhwa. The image is included as a local
asset; the app does not download random images or require an image API.

Prompt used:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: portrait background image for a
> Flutter manga and manhwa reading club app. Create one gorgeous original
> manga/manhwa collage in a 2:3 portrait composition, an immersive editorial
> comic page filling the canvas. Four asymmetric panels with bold black
> diagonal gutters: a silver-haired adult swordsman in a dark coat under a
> crescent moon, an adult female mage with flowing dark hair surrounded by
> coral-red sparks, a rainy futuristic alley with neon reflections, a distant
> fantasy fortress and black birds. Clean expressive inked faces, fine manga
> screentone texture blended with luminous Korean fantasy webtoon coloring.
> Restrained palette of charcoal black, smoky navy, ivory, electric coral-red
> accents. Visually detailed upper two-thirds; lower third fades naturally into
> dark atmospheric ink so login form text can sit on top in the app. Elegant
> dramatic lighting. Portrait art only, edge to edge. No lettering, no speech
> bubbles, no watermarks, no logos, no interface, no phone frame. Original
> characters.
